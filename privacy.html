Privacy policy

Turbo Transfer Hub · last updated 25 November 2026

Turbo Transfer Hub ("the app") is a desktop app that runs on your Mac. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

The short version

The app runs entirely on your Mac. Your files move directly between your Mac and the storage services you connect. Your files, account details and sign-in tokens are never sent to us or to anyone else, and we do not run any server that receives your data.

Data the app accesses

  • Your files and folders in the locations you connect (for example Google Drive). The app reads their names, sizes, dates and contents only to show them to you, to preview them, and to copy, move, rename or delete them when you ask.
  • Sign-in tokens for services you connect (for example the token Google gives the app after you sign in), and any passwords or access keys you enter for other services.
  • Your Google account email address, shown in the app so you can tell your connections apart.

How data is stored

  • Sign-in tokens, passwords and keys are stored only on your Mac, encrypted with the macOS Keychain.
  • Transfer history and settings are stored only on your Mac.
  • The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and sends no data to the app's developer.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google Drive data is used only to provide the file-transfer features you use in the app.
  • It is not transferred to anyone, except to the destinations you choose for your transfers.
  • It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your data.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In the app, open Connections and remove a connection; its token is deleted from your Mac.
  • You can also revoke the app's access to Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the app and its folder in ~/Library/Application Support/Turbo Transfer Hub removes all of its data from your Mac.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: turbotransferhub@gmail.com
