Turbo Transfer Hub

Fast, verified file transfers for the Mac

Turbo Transfer Hub is a desktop app for macOS that copies and moves files between any two places: the Mac's own disks and external drives, Amazon S3 and S3-compatible storage, Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, Azure Blob Storage, SFTP, FTP and WebDAV servers, and web download links.

What it does
  • Browse two locations side by side and copy or move files between them.
  • Send files from many places to many destinations at once.
  • Download large files over several parallel connections.
  • Check every file with checksums so copies are exact.
  • Preview videos, images, audio and documents before transferring.
  • Watch folders and send new files automatically.

Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, you sign in on Google's own page in your web browser. Turbo Transfer Hub never sees your Google password. The app uses access to your Drive only to show your files and folders and to copy, move, rename or delete the files you choose, at your request. See the privacy policy for details.
